FaraPDF Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ashkan Khodayarkhani Hamedani, operating under Rubacava Studio ("FaraPDF", "we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the FaraPDF mobile application, the FaraPDF website, and related services, collectively referred to as the "Service".

FaraPDF is a privacy-focused PDF toolkit. This policy applies to the Android application identified by package name com.farapdf.app, equivalent releases on other platforms, and the website or policy page operated for FaraPDF.

By using the Service, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, do not use the Service. This policy is intended to be read together with the Google Play User Data policy and the privacy policies of third-party services used by the Service.

1. What FaraPDF Does

FaraPDF provides on-device tools for editing, redacting, merging, splitting, converting, scanning, compressing, resizing, rotating, protecting, signing, numbering, and watermarking PDF files.

It also allows you to import images or PDF documents, create output files, and save or share those files at your request.

2. Information Processed by FaraPDF

PDFs, Images, Signatures, and Other File Content

When you choose a file, take a document photo, draw a signature, add a watermark, enter a password, or create a redaction, the relevant content is processed locally on your device by the application.

FaraPDF does not upload the contents of your documents, photos, signatures, passwords, or generated files to servers operated by us.

FaraPDF does not inspect document content for advertising, profiling, analytics, or training purposes. File content is transferred to another application only when you explicitly use your device's sharing or saving functionality.

If a file contains personal information about another person, you are responsible for having the authority to process and share that file.

Device Storage and Temporary Files

With your action and, where required, your permission, FaraPDF can read files you select and write output files to device storage, including application cache and Documents folders such as Documents/FaraPDF.

Temporary working data may remain in the application's cache until the operating system clears it or you clear the application's storage.

Files saved outside the cache remain under your control and can be deleted using your device's file manager.

Camera and Photo Access

The document scanner can use your device's camera or photo picker when you request it. Images are used to crop, enhance, rotate, and convert selected pages into PDF documents locally.

We do not receive or retain those images on our servers.

Data FaraPDF Does Not Request

FaraPDF does not request access to your contacts, phone or call logs, SMS messages, microphone, precise location, biometric records, or list of installed applications.

Google may independently derive an approximate location from an IP address for advertising purposes, as described in the advertising section below.

Preferences and Consent Choices

FaraPDF stores limited settings on your device, such as language and appearance preferences.

If advertising consent is requested, your consent status and related choices may also be stored locally by Google's consent and advertising components.

These settings do not create a FaraPDF account.

Support Communications

If you contact us, we may receive your email address, name, message, attachments, and any information you choose to include.

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, investigate a support issue, protect the Service, or comply with a legal obligation.

Please do not send confidential PDF documents unless necessary for your support request.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

FaraPDF uses Google Mobile Ads, also known as AdMob, to display advertisements.

AdMob is a third-party service operated by Google LLC and its affiliates. When an advertisement request is made, Google and its partners may process information such as:

  • The Android Advertising ID or another advertising identifier available through the operating system.
  • IP address and general network information, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Device and application information, including device model, operating system, application version, language, and connectivity information.
  • Information about advertisement requests, impressions, interactions, diagnostics, security, and fraud prevention.
  • Information stored or accessed by the advertising SDK, subject to your device settings and consent choices.

Google may use this information to deliver, measure, personalize, secure, and improve advertising services.

FaraPDF does not provide Google with the contents of your PDFs, scanned pages, signatures, passwords, or generated files.

Document content is not used to select advertisements and is not included in advertisement requests.

Depending on your region, advertisements may be contextual or personalized.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use a Google-certified consent management platform, such as Google's User Messaging Platform, where required.

You can withdraw or change your consent through the privacy options available in the application or through the advertising controls provided by your device.

For more information, review:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads privacy information

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The AdMob SDK and its partners may use advertising identifiers, SDK storage, local storage, and similar technologies to deliver, measure, secure, and personalize advertisements where permitted.

This privacy policy page does not set FaraPDF advertising cookies. GitHub Pages or linked third-party websites may process ordinary browser information or request logs according to their own policies.
