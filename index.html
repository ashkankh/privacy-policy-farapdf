2. What FaraPDF does

FaraPDF provides on-device tools for opening, editing, redacting, merging, splitting, converting, scanning, compressing, resizing, rotating, protecting, signing, numbering, and watermarking PDF files. Features may vary by platform and version.

3. Information processed

Files and document content

When you select a PDF or image, scan a document, draw a signature, enter a password, add a watermark, or create a redaction, the relevant content is processed locally by the app or browser. In the reviewed implementation, we do not send document contents, scanned pages, signatures, passwords, or generated files to servers operated by FaraPDF.

Files can nevertheless leave your device if you explicitly use the operating system's save, open, backup, cloud, email, or sharing features. The receiving application or provider then handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. You are responsible for having the authority to process and share documents that contain another person's information.

Local storage and temporary data

FaraPDF stores settings such as language, appearance, and local consent-related state on your device. It may store uploaded fonts in browser IndexedDB or application storage so that you can reuse them. Temporary working files may remain in cache or application storage until the operating system or you delete them. Device backups may include application data according to your device settings.

Camera and photo access

The scanner uses the camera or a system photo/document picker only after you request the feature and grant any required permission. Images are used for scanning, cropping, enhancement, rotation, and PDF creation on the device. We do not receive or retain those images on our servers. Denying permission leaves the other features available where the platform permits.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The reviewed Android native release uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display interstitial advertisements. The reviewed web and iOS code path does not request AdMob ads; this must be updated if those platforms are monetized later.

AdMob and its partners may process an advertising identifier, IP address and general network information, approximate location derived from IP, device and app details, ad requests, impressions, interactions, diagnostics, security and fraud-prevention information, and identifiers or local storage used by the SDK. Google may use this information to serve, measure, personalize where allowed, secure, and improve advertising. We do not send document content to AdMob and do not use document content for ad targeting.

Where required in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use Google's User Messaging Platform/Google-certified consent mechanism before requesting ads. You can revisit the available ad-privacy controls in the app's Settings screen. Consent is not the legal basis for processing that is not based on consent. Google may be an independent controller for some processing; see Google's partner-services explanation, Google Privacy Policy, and AdMob privacy information.

Remote font catalogue and font files

When you use the optional online font catalogue or choose a font that is not bundled with the app, the app may request font metadata or font files from the Google Fonts repository, including files served through raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/. Those requests can reveal your IP address, browser/device information, requested font family, and ordinary request logs to GitHub or its providers. No PDF or image content is included in those font requests. You can avoid this processing by using bundled fonts or not using the online font catalogue. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Support

If you email us, we receive the email address, name, message, attachments, and other information you choose to provide. We use it to answer you, investigate a support or security issue, handle privacy requests, and comply with law. Do not send confidential documents unless necessary; redact or minimize them where possible.

Information we do not intentionally request

FaraPDF does not intentionally request contacts, call logs, SMS, microphone, precise location, biometric records, or a list of installed applications. The Android app does request Internet access for advertising and optional font retrieval, and camera access for scanning. The operating system and third-party SDKs may process technical information as described above.
