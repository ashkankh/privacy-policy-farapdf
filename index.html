1. What FaraPDF does

FaraPDF provides on-device tools for editing, redacting, merging, splitting, converting, scanning, compressing, resizing, rotating, protecting, signing, numbering, and watermarking PDF files. It also lets you import images or PDFs, create output files, and save or share those files at your request.

2. Information processed by FaraPDF

PDFs, images, signatures, and other file content

When you choose a file, take a document photo, draw a signature, add a watermark, enter a password, or create a redaction, the relevant content is processed locally on your device by the application. FaraPDF does not upload the content of your documents, photos, signatures, passwords, or generated files to servers operated by us.

FaraPDF does not inspect document content for advertising, profiling, analytics, or training purposes. File content is transferred to another application only when you explicitly use Android's or your device's share/save functionality.

If a file contains personal information about another person, you are responsible for having the authority to process and share that file. FaraPDF does not become the controller of personal information merely because you choose to process it on your device.

Device storage and temporary files

With your action and, where required, your permission, FaraPDF can read files you select and write output files to device storage, including application cache and Documents folders such as Documents/FaraPDF. Temporary working data may remain in the application's cache until the operating system clears it or you clear the app's storage. Files saved outside the cache are controlled by you and can be deleted with your device's file manager.

Camera and photo access

The document scanner can use the device camera or the photo picker when you request it. Images are used to crop, enhance, rotate, and convert your selected pages into a PDF locally. We do not receive or retain those images on our servers.

Data FaraPDF does not request

FaraPDF does not request access to your contacts, phone or call logs, SMS, microphone, precise location, biometric records, or list of installed apps. The app does not run document processing in the background. Google may independently derive a general location from an IP address for advertising purposes as described in the AdMob section.

Preferences and consent choices

FaraPDF stores limited settings on your device, such as language and appearance preferences. If advertising consent is requested, the advertising consent status and related choices may also be stored locally by the Google consent and advertising components. These settings are not an account and are not sent to FaraPDF.

Support communications

If you contact us, we may receive your email address, name, message, attachments, and any information you choose to include. We use this information only to respond, investigate a support issue, protect the Service, or meet a legal obligation. Please do not send confidential PDF content unless it is necessary for your request.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

FaraPDF uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party service operated by Google LLC and its affiliates. When an ad request is made, Google and its partners may process information such as:

  • the Android Advertising ID or another advertising identifier made available by the operating system;
  • IP address and general network information, which may be used to estimate a general location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version, language, and screen or connectivity information;
  • information about ad requests, impressions, interactions, diagnostics, security, and fraud prevention; and
  • information stored or read by the advertising SDK, subject to the choices and settings available on your device and in the consent flow.

AdMob data is used by Google to deliver, measure, limit, personalize, secure, and improve advertising and related services. FaraPDF does not provide Google with the contents of your PDFs, scanned pages, signatures, passwords, or generated files, and we do not use document content to select ads. Ad requests and consent signals may use the internet and are transmitted by the SDK using the security protections provided by Google; document bytes are not included in those ad requests.

Depending on your region, ads may be contextual or personalized. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use a Google-certified consent management platform, such as Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where required. The consent flow is shown before a relevant ad request, and personalized advertising is not requested until a valid legal basis exists. You can withdraw or change your consent through the privacy-options entry point in the app or through the controls provided by your device. You can also manage advertising identifier and ad personalization settings in Android settings.

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, Google Privacy Policy, and Google Mobile Ads privacy information.

Cookies and similar technologies

FaraPDF does not use document content in cookies or similar technologies. The AdMob SDK and its partners may use advertising identifiers, SDK storage, local storage, and similar technologies to deliver, limit, measure, secure, and personalize ads where permitted. This policy page itself does not set FaraPDF advertising cookies. GitHub Pages or a linked third-party site may process ordinary browser storage or request logs under the host's own privacy policy.
